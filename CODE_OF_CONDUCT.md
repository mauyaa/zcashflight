# Code of conduct

ZcashFlight is a privacy tool, and the people who work on it deserve the same respect for their privacy and dignity as its users.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Welcome newcomers, and explain rather than dismiss.
- Keep discussions on topic and in public channels, unless they involve someone's safety or a security issue.
- Respect people's privacy: don't share anyone's personal information, wallet addresses or transaction details without their permission.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to harass someone.
- Posting real keys, seed phrases, addresses or customer data, yours or anyone else's. Use `zcashflight synth` for test values.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the account listed on the repository, and say what happened. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block accounts that break these rules.
